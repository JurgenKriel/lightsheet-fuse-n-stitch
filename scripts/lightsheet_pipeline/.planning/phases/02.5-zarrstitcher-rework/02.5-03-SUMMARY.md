## What was built

Filled in `08_stitch.py`'s `stage_blend()` stub (placed there by plan 02) with the full multiview-stitcher blending implementation, plus two affine helpers used to reattach Stage 1's registered transforms onto fresh per-Z-slab tile sims.

End-state: `08_stitch.py` now supports both `--stage register` (plan 02) and `--stage blend` (this plan). The Stage 2 entry point reads `stitch_positions.json`, opens `fused_direct.zarr` in `mode="r+"`, iterates over (T, C, Z-chunks), reapplies the registered transform to each per-tile sim, calls `multiview_stitcher.fusion.fuse(..., fusion_func=fusion.weighted_average_fusion, blending_widths={"z":0,"y":144,"x":144}, output_stack_mode="union", ...)`, materialises the dask result, and writes uint16 slabs at absolute Z indices.
